The family savings circle, across continents.

01 · The circle

A tontine is a circle.

Every month, everyone puts in the same amount. Every month, one member takes home the whole pot. It works because everyone knows each other.

02 · The scattering

Then the family moved apart.

Six siblings. Five of them now live on five continents. The circle still holds, but it stretches across thousands of kilometres.

03 · The currencies

Five currencies. Rates that move every day.

How many pesos is Emma's share worth this month? Who does the maths, and at which rate?

04 · The friction

Every transfer costs. Nobody sees who paid.

Bank fees, FX fees, days of waiting. And trust comes down to screenshots posted in the group chat.

05 · The answer

Trust Circle closes the loop on Stellar.

Everyone pays the same value, 100 USDC, converted from their own currency by a Stellar path payment. A Soroban contract holds the pot and pays one member each month, in their own currency.
